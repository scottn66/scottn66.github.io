About These Reports

Each report models a 4.5 kW residential rooftop system using irradiance and weather data from the NREL Developer Network (PVWatts v8 API). Financial projections use current California NEM 3.0 tariff schedules from PG&E, the 30% federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC), and real retail electricity rates.

Viability scores (0–100) combine levelized cost of energy (LCOE), simple payback period, net present value (NPV), solar resource quality, panel orientation, and local market maturity into a single weighted index. A score of 65 or above is rated "Good" for most homeowners.

Data sources: NREL PVWatts, EIA utility rate databases, and DSIRE incentive records. Built for DATA 201 at Cal Poly Humboldt.
