What this is

A transparent answer to “is rooftop solar worth it here?” — not a sales funnel. Production comes from NREL PVWatts. California IOU export credits use NEM 3.0 avoided-cost rates (~$0.055/kWh). Oregon currently offers 1:1 net metering. Install costs and market maturity come from Berkeley Lab Tracking the Sun (2.6 million residential records).

The viability score (0–100) is 25% solar resource, 50% economics (LCOE, payback, NPV), 15% site fit, and 10% local market maturity. Scores of 65+ are “Good” for a typical homeowner under these assumptions. ZIP lookup on this page is a California centroid model; city reports use a live PVWatts pull for a representative ZIP.

Outside the US, NREL can still estimate production. Financials need a local electricity rate — the open-source app accepts that as an input and turns off the US federal ITC.

Started as a San Jose State DATA 201 project. Maintained by Scott Nelson.

Independent research model — not financial, tax, or installation advice. Shading, your actual tariff, and installer quotes can change these numbers.
